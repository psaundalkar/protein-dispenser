What this is

A from-scratch control board that replaces the Hefronics controller + BESTEP relay bank in one PCB of the same functional footprint: 8 relay channels, heater triac/SSR drive, RS485/Modbus, sensor inputs. Designed around standard, in-stock parts. Fabricate at JLCPCB / PCBWay / Aisler.

Safety envelope: this board is all low voltage. 230 V AC is switched externally by the Fotek SSR-25DA — mains never enters this PCB except the two SSR control wires.

Design rules & fabrication notes

Stackup & fab

  • 2 layers, 1.6 mm FR-4, 1 oz copper — sufficient; no mains on board
  • Min trace 0.25 mm signal, 1.0 mm for +12V and relay coil bus
  • Board outline: derive from relay spacing (8 × 20 mm pitch = 160 mm min width)
  • Relay coil snubber diodes D5..D12 adjacent to each coil pin
  • Keep SSR drive (J4) away from NTC analog traces — route on opposite side

Safety

  • 230 V never on this PCB. Heater mains switching is entirely inside the external Fotek SSR-25DA
  • Relay contacts switch 12 V DC loads only (augers, solenoids) — verify each load's coil rating
  • Add a 5 A fuse on the +12V output bus if loads total > 3 A
  • PE/earth bonding stays on the chassis, not this board
  • Isolate the RS485 ground from the load ground bus if a long cable run is used

Thermal

  • LM2596S dissipates ~ (VIN−12)×I. At 24 V in / 2 A out → 24 W ×... → use a heatsink tab or drop to 12 V input
  • Prefer 12 V input if you keep the LM2596; at 24 V in, replace with an MP1584/SY8205 buck module
  • ULN2803: ~1 V × 8 × 70 mA = 0.6 W worst case; copper pour under it

Bring-up checklist

  • Power J1 with 12 V current-limited to 200 mA — verify +12 V and +5 V rails
  • Flash via J2 SWD; blink PC13
  • Drive each relay individually; verify with a meter on J8
  • Verify SSR drive at J4 swings 0 → 12 V
  • Read both NTCs at 25°C — expect ~10 kΩ / mid-scale ADC
  • Loopback test on J3 (A↔B shorted) for Modbus

How to turn this into a fabricated board

  • 1. Import hardware/pcb/controller.net into KiCad 7/8 (File → Import → Netlist) — the component list, values, and footprints load automatically.
  • 2. Draw the schematic from the net table above into KiCad Eeschema, or skip straight to layout using the netlist + this block diagram.
  • 3. Layout with the design rules on the left, then run DRC.
  • 4. Export Gerbers + drill (KiCad → Fabrication Outputs) and upload to JLCPCB / PCBWay / Aisler.
  • 5. Verify the bring-up checklist before connecting any real loads.

The netlist is importable as-is; the schematic symbols are standard KiCad library parts. Footprints named in the netlist are from the official KiCad library, so they resolve without extra downloads.
