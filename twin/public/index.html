Subsystems as identified in the assets

  • 5× auger hoppers — geared 12 V DC motors, volumetric dosing (≈3.1 g/rev calibrated)
  • Cup dispenser — solenoid + turntable indexing, cup-present microswitch
  • Mixer head — in-cup blending, stainless block over the cup station
  • Hot water — boiler tank, 240 V element via Fotek SSR-25DA, dispensing solenoid
  • Cold water — vapour-compression loop: compressor → condenser → capillary → evaporator bath, condenser fan
  • Water supply — 2×20 L jugs, silicone tubing, check + solenoid valves
  • Control — Hefronics RS485/Modbus board, 8-ch BESTEP relay board, 8-ch DIP delay module, 5 top actuator boxes
  • Power — Mean Well LRS-100-12 (8.5 A) & LRS-100-24 (4.5 A), LM2596S buck
  • HMI — Android touchscreen kiosk (A8PRTC25080527) + order app
